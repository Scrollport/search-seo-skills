# Evidence-led Content Brief 3.0.1 — reverification

12 October 2026. Status: **Verified; review due 2026-11-11.** Internal rehearsal; not Customer-proven and not client-reviewed.

## Public rehearsal brief

- Site and topic: a public privacy-focused web-analytics product; seed topic `cookieless analytics`.
- Audience: UK small-business owners and marketing leads choosing website analytics.
- Objective and action: qualified free-trial sign-ups after the reader checks UK consent rules.
- Market: United Kingdom, English. Exclusions: no legal advice, no named-competitor claims, no vendor statistics as fact.

Chosen because it is a real, non-sensitive buyer question where vendor assertions and current regulator guidance can disagree, which tests source roles and the claim ledger.

## What was checked

- Both declared tools were discovered by intent and inspected before use; both were live.
- The current keyword-overview contract bills a base task plus a per-keyword charge and makes the SERP summary opt-in (`include_serp_info`, default false). Version 3.0.1 tells the agent to request it and to estimate from the per-keyword charge; this rehearsal used both. The planned maximum was $0.027600 against the $0.050000 ceiling.
- One five-topic batch returned four topics with volume, difficulty, intent, competition and SERP item types; one phrase was not in the provider database and was left unvalidated. SERP snapshot dates ranged from 2023 to September 2026 and were recorded with each topic.
- One five-result source search returned five pages from the UK regulator (ICO), including its guidance finalised on 29 April 2026 and one older page that states it reflects previous guidance.

## Result

**Pass.** The brief selected `cookieless analytics` (volume 40, difficulty 9, informational with secondary commercial intent) over the higher-volume `google analytics alternative` (210, difficulty 63, AI overview present) on audience and intent fit, and kept the latter as a supporting section. It produced an eight-claim ledger with source roles, labelled the older regulator page as superseded, an eight-section outline mapped to ledger items or named first-party inputs, exclusions and a completion checklist. One exception-condition check is assigned to the editor because the detailed chapter was not among the returned sources.
