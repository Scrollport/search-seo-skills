## Public rehearsal brief

A public hosted web-analytics SaaS, with two named business competitors, in the United Kingdom / English market. The objective was free-trial sign-ups and no first-party analytics were supplied. Chosen to add a software business to the earlier trade-business rehearsal, and because its brand and competitors' names are ordinary English words, which tests commercial-fit exclusion on noisy samples.

## What was checked

- All six declared tools were discovered and inspected; all were live. Current inspected prices: ranked keywords and keyword overview $0.016800 per call plus $0.000200 per row or keyword, Google search $0.002500 (up from $0.001400 at the last verification), page extraction $0.003300, fallback extraction $0.002500. The planned maximum was $0.169000 against the $0.500000 ceiling.
- Commercial fit came from the inspected home page (offer, audiences, trial conversion).
- Three 100-row samples, ordered by search volume as chosen before sampling. Most rows were brand, dictionary-word or unrelated matches and were excluded before paid validation. Sampled absence was reported as not observed.
- A ten-phrase overview kept demand, difficulty, intent and SERP item types with their snapshot dates.
- Six current UK searches covered intent and page ownership. Seven page extractions inspected target, rival and owner pages. The regulator page, and two owned pages used for context, were reused from the companion AI visibility run without a new charge. No fallback extraction was needed.

## Result

**Pass.** Three decisions, each with buyer task, demand source, inspected owner page, exact edit or missing evidence, owner, effort and completion check:

1. **Update** an existing 2020 "alternatives" list. It is commercial intent with difficulty 6, ranks 34 in the sample, and the current UK results include comparison lists dated 2026.
2. **Update** an existing cookie-banner guide with UK rules. The guide has no UK, PECR or regulator content; the regulator's statistical-purposes conditions were inspected.
3. **Hold** a high-demand tool phrase. A matching tool page already exists and appears recently dated, so the smallest missing evidence is its index status, not a new page.

A commercial agency phrase was deferred because the live results are how-to guides while the owner is a product page. Head terms and uninspected comparison pages were deferred with reasons.
