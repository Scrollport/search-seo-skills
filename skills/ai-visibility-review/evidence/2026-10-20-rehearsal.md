# Verification — 20 October 2026

Version 4.0.0 passed complete workflow rehearsal and production qualification
on Scrollport's public website. This is internal development evidence, not
independent customer proof or evidence of improved search visibility.

## Method rehearsal

The operator completed a baseline and a same-day recheck on Scrollport's public
website under an explicitly approved $1 total wholesale cap. Each checkpoint
contained five buyer questions, three rounds on ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI
Mode, nine separate by-name answers and five separate ChatGPT source checks:
59 observations per checkpoint, 118 in total. Two generic positive controls and
four public-page reads completed the bounded qualification.

ChatGPT and page reads used the production consumer interface. The two new
Cloro tools used isolated candidate adapters because their public listings were
not yet deployed. All original costs were reconciled to supplier evidence or
the production run-economics API: $0.367600 wholesale in total. The portable
reports distinguish customer wallet receipts from isolated qualification
expenses; wholesale and retail must not be added as if they were two expenses.

Both checkpoints rendered successfully as private HTML, CSV and JSON. Exact
questions, markets, request settings, rounds and coverage matched. ChatGPT
reported a model version; the other engines did not. Their unknown versions
remain a limit on comparison. One original connection interruption was
recovered from the same paid intent with no new debit; its failure and recovery
were retained. The second sample completed without that interruption.

The report separated all buyer measurements from by-name and source passes,
linked proposed actions to observations, checked selected product claims
against official pages, and produced three concrete draft actions. A browser
inspection confirmed the report's layout and visible evidence boundaries.
No website edit, outreach message, recurring schedule or visibility-improvement
claim was made.

## Production qualification

Both added Cloro tools were deployed, passed accepted Admin Tests with useful
answers, and were published before the consumer-interface qualification.
The final report contains 59 production observations: 45 buyer answers, nine
by-name answers and five separate ChatGPT source checks. It reuses the earlier
same-day 23 ChatGPT observations and four page reads, without paying for them
again, and adds 36 observations through the public production MCP.

All 36 original Cloro intents completed and every answer was reviewed for
usefulness, language, claims and citations. One Gemini answer exceeded the
original two-minute deadline. The deployed timeout/recovery fix recovered the
original answer and supplier receipt without another paid request or duplicate
wallet charge. The remaining observations completed with the revised allowance.
This bounded sample does not establish an uptime guarantee.

The complete report shows 15 usable buyer observations per engine. Scrollport
was mentioned, recommended and cited in 0/15 on each engine; these counts stay
separate. By-name answers recognised the business, while two Gemini answers
overstated its exclusion of connected apps and one AI Mode answer implied an
automatic-refill feature not established by the reviewed wallet documentation.
The report records exact claim quotes beside the checked official facts.

Three actions are supported by opened pages and the actual observations:
a concrete company-research example, a Claude Code tool-spending walkthrough,
and a lower-priority clarification pitch to a cited third-party article.
The pitch remains unsent. Private HTML, CSV and JSON were generated and the
production report was visually checked in a browser.

Actual wallet charges represented by the final report are **$0.264400**.
The total supplier-wholesale cost across the earlier method rehearsal,
positive controls, Admin Tests and production qualification is **$0.458800**,
within the approved $1 total test cap. These figures describe different scopes
and must not be added together. No aggregate customer-wallet approval ceiling
was recorded for this internal exercise; the report explicitly distinguishes
that from the wholesale authorization.

Supplier receipts, wallet settlement and the Development classification were
verified. All costs remain recorded; no unknown-cost write-off was used.
The consumer wallet had no unresolved holds after qualification.

Gemini and AI Mode did not report model versions. Same-day variation, unknown
versions and the small convenience sample limit interpretation. No website
change, outreach, recurring monitor, customer-proof or causal improvement
claim is part of verification. Raw answers, private receipts, access links
and account identifiers are excluded from this public repository.

## Report boundary patch 4.0.1

The website export review identified two report-helper gaps: undeclared nested
metadata could enter portable output, and a reduced declared plan could appear
complete without an explicit scope note. Strict object fields now reject raw
metadata before any output files are written. The first screen independently
flags omitted engines, fewer than five questions and fewer than three rounds;
matched reduced plans remain comparable only within their disclosed scope.

All three retained 59-observation checkpoints were rendered again without new
API calls. Execution-environment notes, recovery timestamps and isolated-cost
notes remain in separate private provenance files alongside the unchanged
original checkpoints; their existing scope notes still explain the qualification
context. Observations, quotes, citations, receipts and recorded costs were
unchanged. The baseline/recheck comparison remains valid; the production report
still records $0.264400 in wallet charges and total qualification wholesale stays
$0.458800. All 24 Node tests, including 15 Python behavior tests, and export build
pass. Synthetic checks prove unknown fields cannot create output or print their
values, and that a vague scope note cannot conceal a reduced plan.
